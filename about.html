About SciMSPT

Science, Matter, Physics & Time — A research platform transforming peer-reviewed breakthroughs into investable frontier computing startups.

What is SciMSPT?

SciMSPT (Science, Matter, Physics & Time) is a research-to-investment platform that systematically extrapolates frontier computing opportunities from peer-reviewed scientific breakthroughs published in ScienceDaily across six domains:

  • Matter & Energy — Quantum materials, superconductors, energy storage, hydrogen systems
  • Space & Time — Astrophysics, fusion energy, temporal mechanics, orbital infrastructure
  • Computers & Math — AI/ML algorithms, quantum computing, photonic computing
  • Health & Medicine — Biotech, genomics, DNA storage, neural interfaces
  • Mind & Brain — Neuroscience, cognition, AGI safety, brain-computer interfaces
  • Living Well — Sustainability, green energy, environmental technology

The platform produces weekly digests, research shorts, Monte Carlo-validated startup portfolios, and investor foresight reports — all grounded in real scientific publications.

Research Methodology

Our systematic approach transforms academic breakthroughs into investment-ready startup theses.

The 5-Step Extrapolation Process

1
Breakthrough Identification
Monitor ScienceDaily RSS feeds across 6 domains for peer-reviewed breakthroughs with commercial potential signals.
2
Signal Classification
Categorize each breakthrough by S/M/P/T axis coordinates, maturity level, and investment readiness indicators.
3
Venture Extrapolation
Map scientific advances to fundable business models with TAM, TTM, and competitive moat analysis.
4
Financial Modeling
Build NPV/IRR projections with Monte Carlo simulation (10,000 runs) for statistical validation.
5
Portfolio Integration
Position startups within the SciMSPT microservices platform architecture for operational synergy.
