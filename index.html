About SciMSPT

Scientific Mesh Startup Portfolio

SciMSPT operates at the intersection of frontier science and venture capital. We identify and accelerate technologies that will define the computing landscape from 2025 to 2045 and beyond.

Our methodology combines deep technical due diligence with probabilistic financial modeling. Each portfolio company is mapped against four scaling-wall pillars—Energy, Data Scarcity, Transformer Limits, and AGI Safety—to ensure systematic coverage of AI infrastructure risks and opportunities.

The scientific mandala represents our holistic approach: integrating insights from mathematics, physics, materials science, and computer science into a coherent investment thesis.
