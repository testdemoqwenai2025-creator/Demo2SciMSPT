Company Overview

Temporal Dynamics Research (TDR) is commercializing attosecond spectroscopy—the ability to observe and control electron motion in real-time. Founded in 2024 by University of Cambridge physicists who developed a breakthrough laser system capable of generating light pulses lasting only 50 attoseconds (10⁻¹⁸ seconds), TDR is opening a new frontier of "electron cinematography" that will transform chemistry, materials science, and electronics.

The company's technology enables researchers to watch chemical bonds form and break, observe electron transfer in photovoltaic materials, and understand the fundamental mechanisms of superconductivity—all at the natural timescale of electron motion. With 28 employees, 8 enterprise clients processing 1.5M API calls daily, and growing 28% in just 6 months since their seed extension, TDR is the youngest but fastest-growing company in the SciMSPT portfolio.

Products & Applications

  • AttoScope™: Commercial attosecond imaging system ($850K/unit). Enables universities and R&D labs to observe electron dynamics without building custom laser systems.
  • TempoAI™: Machine learning platform that predicts ultrafast experimental outcomes from material structure—reducing measurement time by 90%. SaaS pricing from $15K/month.
  • ElectronOS™: Operating system for controlling attosecond experiments with automated optimization and analysis.

🎯 Scientific First: In June 2026, TDR's system captured the first-ever video of superconductivity forming in real-time, showing electrons pairing up at femtosecond timescales—a breakthrough that could finally unlock room-temperature superconductors.
