Company Overview

TMD Logic Quantum is pioneering the development of quantum computing processors using Transition Metal Dichalcogenides (TMDs) as the foundational qubit material. Unlike traditional superconducting qubits that require near-absolute-zero temperatures, TMD-based qubits offer superior coherence times, higher operational temperatures, and a clearer path to scalable manufacturing.

The company was founded in 2021 by a team of Stanford physicists and materials scientists who discovered that atomically thin layers of TMD materials like MoS₂ and WSe₂ could host robust qubit states with properties superior to existing approaches. Since then, TMD Logic has raised $125M in funding, grown to 145 employees, and achieved multiple technical milestones that position it as a leader in the race for practical quantum advantage.

The Technology Advantage

Why Transition Metal Dichalcogenides?

  • Superior Coherence: TMD qubits maintain quantum states for 450+ microseconds—10-100x longer than competing technologies at similar temperatures
  • Higher Operating Temperature: Function at 1-4K vs. 0.015K for superconducting qubits, dramatically reducing cooling requirements and costs
  • Scalable Fabrication: Leverages standard semiconductor manufacturing techniques compatible with CMOS foundries
  • Intrinsic 2D Structure: Atomic-scale precision enables perfect qubit uniformity across large arrays

🎯 Key Milestone Achieved: In Q2 2026, TMD Logic demonstrated quantum advantage in materials simulation—solving a real-world problem (catalyst optimization) that would take classical supercomputers 10,000 years, in just 4 hours on their 256-qubit processor.

Market & Competition

TMD Logic operates in the highly competitive quantum computing market, estimated to reach $65B by 2030. Key differentiators include:

  1. Material Advantage: While competitors use superconducting circuits (IBM, Google), trapped ions (IonQ), or photonics (PsiQuantum), TMD Logic's approach combines the best coherence times with manufacturing scalability.
  2. Temperature Edge: Operating at 1-4K vs. 15mK reduces cooling costs by ~100x, making deployment feasible in standard data centers.
  3. Error Correction Progress: Demonstrated logical qubit error rates below threshold for fault-tolerant computing—a critical milestone many competitors haven't yet achieved.
