Company Overview

Computational Physics Labs (CPL) is building the operating system for scientific simulation. Their cloud-native platform combines physics-based modeling with machine learning acceleration to enable researchers and engineers to simulate complex systems—from molecular dynamics to climate models—100x faster than traditional methods at a fraction of the cost.

Founded in 2023 by a team of ex-NVIDIA engineers and computational physicists, CPL has quickly become the go-to platform for R&D teams across pharmaceuticals, aerospace, energy, and materials science. With 34 enterprise customers including 3 Fortune 500 companies, processing 2.5 million simulation hours monthly, and growing 56% year-over-year, CPL is democratizing access to supercomputing-level simulation capabilities.

The Platform

CPL's platform consists of three integrated products:

  • SimCore™: Cloud-native physics engine supporting finite element analysis, molecular dynamics, computational fluid dynamics, and quantum chemistry calculations with automatic GPU acceleration.
  • ML-Accel™: Machine learning layer that learns from previous simulations to predict results for similar problems—achieving 10-100x speedup for parametric studies while maintaining DFT-level accuracy.
  • CollabSpace™: Real-time collaboration environment for distributed research teams with version control, visualization tools, and automated reporting.

🎯 Customer Win: A top-5 pharmaceutical company used CPL's platform to reduce drug candidate screening time from 18 months to 6 weeks, accelerating their pipeline by 12x and saving an estimated $50M in R&D costs annually.
