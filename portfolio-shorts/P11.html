Company Overview

Advanced Materials Corp (AMC) is the first company to achieve commercial-scale production of metamaterials—engineered materials with properties not found in nature. Founded in 2022 by Carnegie Mellon materials scientists, AMC has developed proprietary roll-to-roll manufacturing processes that produce invisibility cloaking materials, perfect lenses, and adaptive building facades at costs 100x lower than previous methods.

The company's breakthrough came from combining nanoscale 3D printing with self-assembly techniques to create metamaterial sheets up to 2 meters wide—previously limited to lab-scale samples of a few centimeters. With 31 patents, $12M annual revenue run rate, and contracts spanning defense, aerospace, construction, and consumer electronics, AMC is bringing science fiction materials to reality.

Product Lines

  • InvisiShield™: Radar-invisible coating for military vehicles and aircraft. Reduces radar cross-section by 99%. $45M contract with U.S. Department of Defense.
  • PerfectLens™: Flat lenses with unlimited resolution beyond diffraction limit. Applications in smartphone cameras, microscopes, and AR/VR optics.
  • AdaptiveFacade™: Building envelope material that automatically adjusts transparency and thermal properties based on environmental conditions. Reduces building energy consumption by 40%.
  • AbsorbaCore™: Sound-absorbing panels for noise cancellation in aerospace and automotive applications.

🎯 Manufacturing Milestone: In May 2026, AMC's Pittsburgh facility achieved production of its 10,000th square meter of InvisiShield™ material—demonstrating commercial viability at scale previously thought impossible for metamaterials.
