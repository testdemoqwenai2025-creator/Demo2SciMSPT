Company Overview

CMOS NextGen Semiconductor (CMOS) is the first fabless company to achieve volume production of 2nm Gate-All-Around Field Effect Transistor (GAA-FET) chips. Founded in 2020 by a team of TSMC and Intel veterans who believed they could outpace the semiconductor giants by focusing exclusively on the most advanced node, CMOS has proven that smaller, focused teams can win against industry titans through superior architecture and execution.

The company's chips achieve an unprecedented 500 million transistors per square millimeter—2x denser than TSMC's 3nm process—while maintaining a remarkable 78% yield rate at volume production. With $185M raised, 210 employees including 80 PhD-level engineers, 15 million chips shipped, and growing 178% year-over-year, CMOS is the most valuable company in the SciMSPT portfolio and arguably the most important semiconductor startup in the world.

Technology Leadership

  • GAA-FET Architecture: Nanosheet transistors with gate wrapping around channel on all four sides for perfect electrostatic control—enabling continued voltage scaling below 0.7V.
  • Back-Side Power Delivery: Revolutionary power network routing on chip backside, freeing front-side space for 30% more logic density and eliminating IR drop issues.
  • CFET Roadmap: 3D-stacked complementary FETs planned for 2028 release, targeting 1nm equivalent density with 40% area reduction over current 2nm.
  • High-Mobility Channels: Integrating SiGe for PMOS and InGaAs for NMOS achieving 2-3x drive current improvement over pure silicon.

🎯 Production Milestone: In July 2026, CMOS began volume shipment of 2nm GAA-FET chips to Apple for use in M-series Mac processors and future A-series iPhone chips—marking the first time a fabless startup has supplied leading-edge process technology to a top-tier device maker.
