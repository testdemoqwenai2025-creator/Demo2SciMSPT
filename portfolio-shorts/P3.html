Company Overview

Quantum Materials Inc. (QMI) is revolutionizing energy transmission, quantum computing, and medical imaging through the development of room-temperature superconducting materials. Founded in 2022 by Dr. Sarah Chen and a team of MIT physicists, QMI has achieved what many thought impossible: superconductors that operate at temperatures achievable with standard liquid nitrogen cooling (77K) rather than expensive helium-based systems.

The company's breakthrough came from combining machine learning-driven materials discovery with novel synthesis techniques for copper-oxide based compounds and recently discovered hydride materials under moderate pressure. With 24 patents filed, $45M raised across two funding rounds, and 85 world-class scientists and engineers, QMI is positioned to transform multiple industries simultaneously.

Technology & Products

  • Q-Wire™: Room-temperature superconducting wire for lossless power transmission—reducing grid losses from ~7% to near-zero
  • Q-MRI™: Superconducting magnets operating at 77K enabling cheaper, more accessible MRI machines for developing markets
  • Q-Qubit™: Superconducting qubit interconnects with 100x lower thermal noise than conventional approaches
  • Q-Lev™: Magnetic levitation systems for next-generation high-speed rail and transportation

🎯 Breakthrough Achievement: In January 2026, QMI demonstrated a wire carrying 10 million amps per cm² at 77K—surpassing the previous record by 100x and making commercial power transmission applications economically viable for the first time.
