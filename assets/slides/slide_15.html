Strategic Imperatives

Three Imperatives for SciMSPT

01
Build Abstraction Layers Now

Compute Service must evolve from job scheduler for classical workers into unified abstraction over quantum, photonic, neuromorphic, and classical resources.

02
Invest in Materials Visibility

Platforms that prove chip provenance, track materials origins, integrate with automated wet-lab infrastructure compound advantages as materials scarcity becomes binding.

03
Embed AI Safety First-Class

Hardware-level safety (signed circuits, verifiable proofs, genomic privacy) is structurally more robust than behavioral training. Auth Service becomes central value proposition.

“Companies that act now, while science is still maturing and architecture decisions are still open, will define the next generation of computing infrastructure.”
